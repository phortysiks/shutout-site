Live Activities

Face-off to final, without opening the app.

  • Starts itself at face-off. Your team's card appears as the puck drops, reading “Starting soon” until the game's under way.
  • Every goal, as it's posted. The score rolls over, with the scorer, the assists and the time it went in.
  • Goals come to you. The Dynamic Island opens for each goal, and with your phone locked, your Apple Watch taps your wrist.
  • The final stays put. The result stays on your Lock Screen for an hour after the buzzer.

Widgets

Your team, at a glance.

  • My Team shows your favourite's next game, the one being played, or the latest result, in small or medium.
  • Team follows any club you choose, so rivals and away days get a widget too.
  • Today's Games puts the whole night on one widget: up to four games, your team always among them.
  • A line on your Lock Screen with the next face-off or the live score.

Matches

Every game, in one place.

  • The whole season of Elite League and Challenge Cup games, results and fixtures, opening on today.
  • Filter to your team, or any team, in a tap.
  • Live scores refresh every 15 seconds while games are on.
  • Face-off times in your own time zone, wherever you're watching from.

Game detail

Every goal, every period.

  • Period-by-period scoring, through overtime and shootouts.
  • A timeline of every goal: who scored, who assisted, and when.
  • Turn a game's Live Activity or notifications on or off, right from its page.

Standings

Where your team stands.

  • The Elite League table and the Challenge Cup groups.
  • Games played, record, goal difference and points, with your team highlighted.
  • Updated as soon as the night's games are over.
